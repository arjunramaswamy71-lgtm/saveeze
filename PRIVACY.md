# savEEZE privacy policy

*Last updated: October 22, 2026*

savEEZE is built so that it never needs your data.

## What it does
When you click the savEEZE button on a product page, it loads that same page from the store a few times, once as you and twice without any cookies or sign-in, and compares the prices. All of this happens inside your own browser.

## What it collects
Nothing. savEEZE has no server. It does not send your browsing, prices, location or any other information to us or to anyone else.

## What it stores
Only the state you choose in the popup, saved on your own device so you don't have to pick it again. You can clear it any time by removing the extension.

## Permissions
- **activeTab** and **scripting**: let savEEZE read the product page you're on, and only after you click it.
- **storage**: remembers the state you picked.

savEEZE does not ask to read every website, and it does not run in the background on pages you visit.

## Changes
If a future version adds the optional Fair Price Index, it will be off by default, anonymous, and explained here before it ships.
